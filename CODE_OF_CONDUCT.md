# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and assume the other person is acting in good faith. Discuss
the code, not the person writing it. When you disagree, say what you would do
instead and why.

## Expected behaviour

- Give reasons. "This is wrong" is not a review; "this breaks convergence when
  the previous state is uncertain, see ADR-0003" is.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change that is correct but not worth
  its complexity, and that a contributor may decline to keep iterating.
- Assume the other person knows something you do not, particularly about their
  own deployment.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination on any grounds, publishing
someone's private information, and persistent disruption after being asked to
stop. Also: pressuring anyone to work faster on unpaid work.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainer through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/liuqjjin/cogindex/security/advisories/new),
which is the available private channel on this repository, or by any direct
contact you already have. Reports are handled confidentially.

Responses range from a private word to a permanent ban, and the maintainer will
say which one is being applied and why. Being technically correct does not
exempt anyone from this document.
